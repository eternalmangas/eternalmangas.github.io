# Web Reader

The EternalMangas web reader is available at **[mangapdf.org](https://mangapdf.org)**. It lets you read manga and comics directly in your browser — no app installation or extension needed.

## What It Is

A simple, distraction-free web reader. No accounts, no sign-ups, no clutter. Just open the site and start reading.

## How It Works

- **No account required** — Open [mangapdf.org](https://mangapdf.org) and start browsing immediately.
- **Local storage** — All your data, reading progress, and preferences are stored in your browser. Nothing is sent to or stored on a server.
- **Full catalog** — Access the same 2900+ series available through the extensions and API.
- **Clean reading experience** — Minimal UI focused on reading, without ads or distractions.
